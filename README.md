An original sunlit harbor-city crime adventure for the browser. Help Inez recover a stolen ledger in six linked jobs across the old market, downtown, container port and marina. Take the coral coupe, run the port yard, out-drive the patrols, then broadcast the truth across Costa Luz.

## Play locally

Requires Node.js 22 or newer. No build, install, network access or external assets required.

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open <http://localhost:8080> in desktop Chrome. Or open `game/index.html` directly; all game assets are local.

## Controls

- `WASD` / arrow keys: walk or drive
- `E`: speak, interact, steal a nearby vehicle, or leave your car
- Mouse click: aim and fire the pulse pistol on foot
- `Space`: handbrake
- `Shift`: sprint
- `M`: expand the minimap
- `Esc`: pause/resume

Your game progress saves locally in this browser. Web Audio provides original synthesized sound effects; use the sound button to mute them.

## In the box

- **Six linked jobs** from the market crossing to Pier Seven, the container yard, a marina garage and the Rádio Maré transmitter.
- **A city you can walk and drive**: streets, traffic with lights, pedestrians who scatter, and cars you can take.
- **A wanted level that answers**: patrols spawn, close in, and are lost by breaking line of sight or respraying the car.
- **Side work**: courier contracts at Mercado Courier, free repairs at Sol Garage, respray and plate changes at Chroma, five hidden harbor caches.
- **One save slot** in the browser, written on every story beat and on pause.

## About the world

Costa Luz, its districts, characters, cars, dialogue, art and sounds are original. The world is assembled from local canvas art and static JavaScript modules; there are no third-party assets or runtime network requests.
